Brand Colors

The Gemwhiz palette combines dark neutral tones, warm ivory and copper to create an earthy, handcrafted and slightly mystical identity.

Charcoal #0B0B0B
Warm Ivory #F1ECE4
Copper #B98555
Soft Gray #F4F4F4

Typography

Gemwhiz uses Georgia for headings and Arial or Helvetica for body copy. The combination gives the brand an editorial feel while keeping text easy to read.
